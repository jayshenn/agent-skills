# Ratios and Valuation Reference

## Profitability
- Gross margin = (revenue - cost of goods sold) / revenue
- Operating margin = operating income / revenue
- Net margin = net income / revenue
- ROE = net income / average shareholder equity
- ROA = net income / average total assets

## Liquidity and Solvency
- Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities
- Quick ratio = (current assets - inventory) / current liabilities
- Cash ratio = cash and equivalents / current liabilities
- Debt-to-assets = total debt / total assets
- Interest coverage = EBIT / interest expense

## Efficiency
- Asset turnover = revenue / average total assets
- Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold / average inventory
- Receivables turnover = revenue / average accounts receivable

## Valuation
- PE = price per share / earnings per share
- PB = price per share / book value per share
- PS = market cap / revenue
- EV/EBITDA = enterprise value / EBITDA
- PEG = PE / earnings growth rate

## ETF Branch Metrics
- Expense ratio: annual fee drag (lower is better).
- Tracking error: deviation from benchmark return (lower is better).
- AUM (USD bn): tradability/stability proxy (higher is better).
- Bid-ask spread (%): execution friction (lower is better).
- Average daily volume: liquidity depth (higher is better).
- 1Y/3Y/5Y annualized return: performance persistence.
- Volatility / Max drawdown / Sharpe: risk-adjusted quality.
- Top-10 weight / sector concentration (HHI): concentration risk.

## Consistency Rules
- Do not mix TTM and annual metrics in one ratio set.
- Do not mix GAAP and non-GAAP values in one ratio set.
- State currency and unit in every table.
