# Risk Discipline Rules (MVP)

## Hard Rules
- Do not chase price when bias vs MA5 exceeds 5%.
- If MA5 < MA10 < MA20, avoid long-bias recommendations.
- If RSI is in overbought zone (>= 70), downgrade confidence.
- Every recommendation must include an explicit invalidation condition.

## Position Sizing Guidance
- High conviction: up to 5% starter position.
- Medium conviction: 2%-3% starter position.
- Low conviction: <= 1% or watchlist only.

## Risk Disclosure Requirements
- Distinguish verified data from assumptions.
- Mark unavailable live data as unverified.
- Include at least three downside risk factors.
